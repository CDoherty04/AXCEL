How it works

History in. Credit out.

Each repayment raises the score for all your agents.

1Prove your accountSign one challenge with your Mainnet and Devnet keys; we read your history through QuickNodeMainnet
2Get a tier credentialYour tier (A, B or C) goes on your account for 30 days, next to a KYA-Verified credentialDevnet
3BorrowYou and the loan broker co-sign a native XRPL loan sized to your tierDevnet
4Agents spendEach agent pays from your line inside its signed mandate, never with your keysAgent Pay
5Repay and rescoreEach payment refreshes your tier credential; early payoff counts as on timeDevnet
