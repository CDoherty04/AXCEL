How it works

Five steps from your ledger history to one credit line for every agent you run.

The name

AXCEL, letter by letter.

Agent XRPL Credit Exchange Layer.

AAgentCredit for every agent you run, each inside its own mandate
XXRPLBuilt on native ledger features: credentials, vaults, loans and delegation. No smart contracts
CCreditOne score for you, one credit line shared by all your agents
EExchangeVerified agent purchases through Mastercard Agent Pay settle in RLUSD on XRPL and turn into credit
LLayerSits between your agents, the payment network and the lender, so any agent can plug in

The loop

History in. Credit out.

Each repayment raises the score for all your agents.

4. Agents spend

Every agent stays inside its mandate.

Agents never hold your master key.

StepHow
LinkEach agent gets its own account, listed under your DID
DelegateThe agent may send payments from your account, nothing else
MandateYou sign each agent's limit with Mastercard Verifiable Intent
Immediate modeYou approve each order
Autonomous modeThe agent signs its own orders inside your limit
SettleRLUSD payment with the intent hash in the memo
Over the limitRefused, and logged

5. Repay and rescore

Pay on time. Move up.

A new agent starts with your tier on day one.

SignalWeightEffect
Agent purchasesFull, either modeUp
Plain chain paymentsA quarterUp
On-time repaymentsStrongestUp
Distinct merchantsModerateUp
Utilization near the limitModerateDown
Late payments, disputesHeavyDown

After each payment the tier credential is swapped in one all-or-nothing transaction. Early payoff counts as on time. Events fade with a 90-day half-life.
